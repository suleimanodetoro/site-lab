# Responsible use

Site Lab is for studying how web pages are built, so you can learn their techniques and make your own work.

**What the tool does:**
- It loads pages the way a normal browser does.
- On cookie banners it refuses optional cookies; it never accepts them for you.
- It records the robots.txt result, as advisory information.
- It does not solve CAPTCHAs or get around bot protection.

**Guidance for what you make with it:**
- **Rebuilds are study copies.** Keep them private and local, like the git-ignored `rebuilds/` folder. Don't publish or deploy a clone of someone else's site.
- **Don't ship captured material:** no images, video, fonts, logos or copy belonging to the site's owner. Things you publish should use your own content and assets, or assets whose licence allows it.
- **Borrow techniques, not identity.** A scroll effect, a timing curve or a layout idea is fair to learn from. A brand's look and words are not yours to reuse.
- **Be light on other people's servers.** A capture loads a page roughly 10–20 times. Don't loop it.
- **Captures can contain personal information** that a page displays. Treat capture folders as private.
